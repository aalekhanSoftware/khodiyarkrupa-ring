About Khodiyar Krupa Ring Turning

Khodiyar Krupa Ring Turning is a CNC job work workshop that makes rings and turned components for the automobile, machinery and vehicle industries. We take work on a job work basis — you bring the drawing or sample, we machine the part.

Our CNC machines handle components from 50mm up to 500mm outer diameter. Everyday parts we produce include bushes, spacers, sleeves, flanges, bearing rings and distance pieces used in gearboxes, pumps, axles and industrial machines.

We keep the job simple: correct material, correct dimensions, clean finish, and delivery on the promised date. Whether it is a single prototype or a running batch, each part is checked against the drawing before dispatch.
